A radar chart that rescales with every filter makes a 57 look like a 100. Radar & Spider Chart by TCViz compares suppliers, people or products on several criteria at once, on a scale that starts at zero and does not move, with the axes kept in place and a gap wherever a value is missing. Power BI has no built-in radar chart.

──────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT IT DOES
──────────────────────────────────────────
✅ A common scale from zero, with a minimum and maximum you can fix
✅ Axes keep their order when you filter
✅ Empty values drawn as a gap, never as zero
✅ Measures as axes, no unpivoting
✅ A note when one axis flattens the others, and when values fall outside a fixed scale
✅ Six style templates, including an IBCS style; curved lines, marker shapes, rotation
✅ Click to filter, highlight, bookmarks, drilldown, keyboard navigation and high contrast
✅ The measure's number format, dynamic format strings included; twelve languages
✅ No network requests: everything is computed inside your report
