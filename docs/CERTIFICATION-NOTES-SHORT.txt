WHAT TO CHECK
1. Empty visual: start screen; resize it (three layouts).
2. Sample report: Axis = Criterion, Values = Score (Average), Series = Supplier. Fixed scale
from zero, 3 of 6 suppliers in the free version (the legend says so).
3. Pro, editing without licence: Target = Required level, Small multiples = Family,
Axis group = Area; Scale > Per axis. Watermark appears.
4. Click a point: other visuals filter by that axis and series (every identity of the point).
An axis label or legend item selects the whole axis or series. Click again to clear;
Ctrl+click adds. Bookmark, clear, apply bookmark: the selection comes back.
5. Keyboard: arrows move between points, Enter selects, Esc clears. High contrast theme,
or Format > Accessibility: High contrast On, Line patterns per series, Minimum text size.
6. Format mode: click a shape, the grid, an axis label, the legend or the background.
